Career Atlas privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026.

Career Atlas is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

Read only access to the owner's Gmail (scope gmail.readonly), used to read job alert emails from job sites.

What it does with it

  • Extracts job titles, employers, locations and links from job alert emails.
  • Stores those job details only on the owner's computer.
  • Never sends, deletes, labels or marks email, and never shares Gmail data with anyone.
  • Does not sell data, show ads, or transfer data to third parties.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
